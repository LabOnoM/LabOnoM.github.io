## Visually Inspected Figures
### 1. `_posts/PostAttachedFiles/NatureLifeCycleVsLabLifeCycle.png` (`2,000 × 1,379` px, RGB PNG)
- **Direct Visual Observation**:
  - Two-column circular calendar schematic comparing **(A) Wild *Danio rerio* seasonal lifecycle in India** (`Winter` Jan–Feb cold/dry rice paddies; `Summer` Mar–May air temperatures `>45°C` northern/central India and `35–40°C` west/south; `Monsoon` Jun–Sep flooded floodplains triggering heavy feeding and external spawning; `Post-monsoon` Oct–Dec `20–30°C` receding waters; maturation `~10 months`) against **(B) Controlled laboratory lifecycle** (`28°C` constant water conditions across all 12 months, hatching `~3 dpf`, exogenous feeding by `5 dpf` on paramecium/rotifers -> *Artemia* -> `300–600 µm` pellets, metamorphosis `14–29 dpf`, sexual maturity `~90 dpf` with year-round weekly spawning).

### 2. `_posts/PostAttachedFiles/Zebrafish-evolution-and-range.png` (`1,999 × 1,680` px, RGB PNG)
- **Direct Visual Observation**:
  - Reproduction of textbook `FIGURE 1.1 Zebrafish evolution and range`:
    - **(A)** Cumulative *Danio* species described from `1800` to `2020`, beginning with *Danio rerio* (`Hamilton, 1822`, red arrow) and accelerating after `1995` to `~26` species.
    - **(B)** Satellite relief map of South Asia plotting historical and modern *D. rerio* collection localities across India, Nepal, Bangladesh, and Myanmar across four eras (`1868–1909` green, `1910–1949` yellow, `1950–1989` orange, `1990–2017` red; inset wild adult photograph from `Engeszer et al., 2007`).
    - **(C)** Phylogenetic cladogram (after `McCluskey and Postlethwait, 2015`) resolving *D. rerio* (red) sister to *D. aesculapii* inside the *D. rerio* species group (*D. tinwini*, *D. nigrofasciatus*, *D. kyathit*, *D. aff. kyathit* in blue).
